Verify before you pay

Five checks that settle most disputes in one message.

  • 1Ask for the actual bill. BWSSB bill or society water invoice for that month, not a typed total on WhatsApp.
  • 2Ask how it was split. Per flat, per person, or by meter reading — and how many flats/people it was split across.
  • 3Count the tankers. Number of deliveries × size × rate should match the tanker line. Societies usually keep a gate register.
  • 4Photo the meter. If you have a sub-meter, take a dated photo on the 1st of each month.
  • 5Compare per 1,000 L. Anything above ₹170 / 1,000 L for tanker water needs a reason: distance, summer, or a small load.
